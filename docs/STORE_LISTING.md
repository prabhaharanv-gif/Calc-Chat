## Full description (max 4000 characters, paste as is)

```
Gamaroon is a games app with a private messenger built in.

IMPORTANT: besides the games, this app contains a private one-to-one chat. The chat is hidden on purpose, as a privacy feature chosen by you. You open it by holding a game title (or simply tapping it, if you prefer) and entering your own passcode. Nobody else sees the chat unless you open it.

PLAY
Six classic mini games, free and without ads:
- Snake
- 2048
- Memory
- Tic Tac Toe (against the computer)
- Mines
- Slide puzzle
Your scores are saved on your device.

PRIVATE CHAT
- No sign-up, no phone number, no email address. Every user gets a personal code; share it with one friend to start a one-to-one chat.
- End-to-end encrypted text, photos, voice messages, videos and files. The server only relays encrypted data and cannot read it.
- Messages and media are deleted automatically after 30 minutes and are kept only in the server's memory, never in a database.
- Reply, edit, delete for everyone, react with emoji, or clear the whole chat for both people at any time.
- Sent, delivered and read ticks. An online/offline switch for each chat lets you choose who sees you online.
- A safety number lets you and your friend confirm nobody is listening in, and the app warns you if your friend's key changes.

BUILT FOR PRIVACY
- Silent Auto-Wipe (you can switch it off in Settings): after 3 wrong passcode attempts in a row, the chat data on your device is erased and the chat is deleted on the server. This can remove the conversation for your friend too.
- Screenshots, screen recording and the recent-apps preview are blocked by default (switch in Settings).
- Notifications never show names or message text, only a generic game reminder such as "Your game is waiting!". You can turn the background connection that powers them off in Settings.
- Your passcode is stored only as a salted, slow hash. On Android your chat key is protected with the Android Keystore.

GOOD TO KNOW
- An internet connection is needed to chat. Games work offline.
- Chats are temporary by design. When a message expires, it is gone for everyone.
- You can delete or leave a chat at any time, which deletes its data on the server.
- Not intended for children under 13.

Questions or abuse reports: support@kinest.co.in
```
