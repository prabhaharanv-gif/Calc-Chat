**Title (30):** Calc Chat: Private Chat
**Short description (80):** Private 2-person chat hidden behind a working calculator.

**Full description**
Calc Chat looks and works like a normal calculator. Enter your secret passcode and press = to open a private chat.

NOTE: This app is a private messenger disguised as a calculator. The chat is hidden until you enter your passcode.

- No sign-up, no phone number, no email
- Every user gets a personal code; share it with one friend to start a private chat
- Send text, photos, voice messages and videos
- Messages and media auto-delete after 4 hours and are never stored in a database
- Clear the chat for both people at any time
- 3 wrong passcodes erase your chats
- Screenshots and screen recording are blocked
