Gamaroon – Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{UPDATED}}

In short: no accounts, no ads, no analytics. Messages are end-to-end encrypted on your devices, held only in the server's memory for 30 minutes at most, and never written to a disk or database.

What the app is

Gamaroon is a collection of mini games (Snake, 2048, Memory, Tic Tac Toe, Mines, Slide) that also contains a private one-to-one chat. The chat stays hidden until you open it with the secret gesture and passcode you set. This is a deliberate privacy feature chosen by the user, and it is described in the store listing.

Accounts and personal data

There are no accounts. We do not ask for your phone number, email address, contacts or location. The display name you choose is sent to your friend end-to-end encrypted; the server cannot read it.

What the server processes

  • Messages, photos, voice notes, videos and files. They are encrypted on your device before they are sent. The server only relays encrypted data. It is kept in memory (RAM) and deleted automatically 30 minutes after sending, when either person clears the chat, when a chat is deleted or left, or when the server restarts. It is never written to disk or to a database.
  • Technical data needed to deliver messages: the chat code, a secret that proves membership (stored only as a hash), the two devices' public encryption keys, message times, the approximate size class of each message, sent / delivered / read status and whether a device is online. This data lives in memory and disappears with the chat.
  • IP address. Your connection's IP address is visible to the server and is used in memory to limit abuse (rate limiting). We do not store it and do not keep access logs of chats.

What stays on your device

Your passcode (stored only as a salted, slow hash), your chat code and secret, your encryption keys (on Android the private key is protected with the Android Keystore), your settings and game scores. Messages are never saved on the device.

Security

  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • End-to-end encryption (ECDH key agreement and AES-256-GCM). You can compare a safety number with your friend to detect a man-in-the-middle, and the app warns you if your friend's key changes.
  • Screenshots, screen recording and the recent-apps preview are blocked by default (you can change this in Settings).
  • Silent Auto-Wipe (optional, on by default): after 3 wrong passcode attempts in a row the app erases the chat data on the device and asks the server to delete the chat for both people. This can remove the conversation for your friend too. You can turn it off in Settings.

Notifications and background connection

To tell you that something is waiting while the app is closed, the Android app keeps a small background connection to the server (a foreground service, shown as a low-priority notification). When your friend writes, it shows a generic message such as “Your game is waiting!”. Notifications never contain names or message content. You can turn this off with the “Secret signal” switch in Settings.

Permissions

  • Internet – to reach the server.
  • Notifications – the generic game reminder described above.
  • Foreground service (special use) – keeps one small encrypted connection open so the generic reminder can be shown while the app is closed.
  • Microphone – only when you record a voice message.
  • Photos, videos and files are chosen through the system file picker; the app does not read your gallery.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools. Chat content can only be read by the two people in a chat; the server operator can see only the technical data listed above.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

You can delete a chat or leave it at any time in the app, which deletes its data on the server. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device.
